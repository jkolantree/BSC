# BSC Core v1.5 — executive summary

## The one-sentence result

Within a declared finite candidate model, BSC Core v1.5 gives a precise answer
to “what evidence is missing?”: a report family supports a target exactly when
it separates every candidate pair on which that target differs, so
minimum-cost exact evidence design is a target-relative weighted set-cover
problem.

## What is new in the repository

- a repaired product semantics separating verdict, run outcome, readiness,
  and inapplicable coordinates;
- a finite cyclic readiness-cap theorem and exact cyclic fixture;
- an operational identifiability/factorization theorem;
- an exact finite intervention-design checker with rational arithmetic;
- finite target-oscillation semantics for approximate reports;
- proposed causal-transfer and AI-assisted evidence profiles;
- a selected machine-readable claim registry and executable readiness graph;
- the first non-Q26 BSC Lean project; and
- primary-source prior-art and recent-research maps.

## Exact evidence

| Artifact | Exact result | Ceiling |

| F14 intervention design | Minimum cost `2`; minimizers `direct` and `column + row`; tolerant `direct` oscillation `3/2` | Four-state declared model only |

| Claim package | IDs, verdicts, dependencies, paths, hashes, and cap closure agree | Package integrity and declared semantics, not truth of curator premises |

## Honest claim boundary

This milestone does not establish a full category of BSC morphisms, validate a
physical or causal model, settle mathematical novelty, or convert correlated
AI reviewers into external referees. The immutable v1.4.0 record is unchanged.

The strongest public wording is:

> BSC now has a human-readable and partially kernel-checked finite core for
> readiness closure and claim-relative identifiability, plus exact bounded
> implementations for intervention selection and cyclic cap propagation. Its
> causal and AI evidence layers are proposed profiles. Originality of the
> combined contract remains unknown pending external comparison and review.
