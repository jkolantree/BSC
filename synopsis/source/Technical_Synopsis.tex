\section*{The problem}

Scientific claims often fail not within either of two descriptions, but in the
passage between them: interior state to boundary data, one instrument to
another, a fine model to a coarse model, a learned operator to a dynamical
claim, or a topological invariant to a physical quantity.  The paper develops
Boundary-State Calculus (BSC) as a typed audit calculus for this passage.

BSC is not proposed as an ontology, a unified physical theory, or a proof of
holography, quantum gravity, consciousness, or QCD.  Its unit of evaluation is
one claimed transfer.  That transfer must state what moves, which observables
remain meaningful, what information is destroyed, whether target equations
remain satisfied, which diagrams fail to commute, what physically implements
the passage, and what evidence licenses the result.

\section*{System and boundary discipline}

The paper retains the BSC corpus's original finite-system tuple and its later
measurable repair rather than replacing them retrospectively (\S3, pp.~6--8).
The repaired system separates raw state from exact observational equivalence
and includes controls, outputs, physical context, legal filtration, dynamics,
observation, comparison geometry, viability, transformation registries, and a
boundary ledger.  A companion record supplies reconstruction data, provenance,
and certificate state without silently changing the inherited tuple.
Instrument, controller, observer or reference frame, clock, and calibration
are physical configuration variables, not metadata.

An ideal trace, finite sensor layer, boundary response, reconstruction
relation, and selected extension are kept distinct (\S5, pp.~9--10).  A
boundary response supports an interior claim only relative to a governing
equation, coefficient class, equivalence or gauge class, regularity, data
regime, and stability theorem.

\section*{The transfer record}

The central added object is (\S7, pp.~11--14)
\[
\resizebox{0.96\columnwidth}{!}{$\displaystyle
\mathfrak M_{\ell\to m}=
\bigl(T_{\ell m},T_{\ell m}^{\sharp},K_{\ell m},R_{\ell m},
\Theta_{\ell m},\delta_{\ell m},C_{\ell m},\mathsf{Cert}_{\ell m}\bigr)
$}.
\]
\(T_{\ell m}\) transports states; \(T_{\ell m}^{\sharp}\) pulls target
observables in the reverse direction.  \(K_{\ell m}\) is post-processing or a
simulation channel.  \(R_{\ell m}\) records target-equation failure.
\(\Theta_{\ell m}\) is a declared naturality defect.  \(\delta_{\ell m}\) is
directed Blackwell--Le Cam deficiency: the best error when the source
experiment simulates the target.  \(C_{\ell m}\) records carrier, controller,
instrument, reference frame, clock, resources, and boundary conditions.
\(\mathsf{Cert}_{\ell m}\) binds assumptions, tolerances, sources, proof or
execution artifacts, hashes, open obligations, and status.

These coordinates do not collapse into one score.  Equation residual,
observation defect, statistical deficiency, quotient loss, and viability
failure occupy different spaces and obey different propagation laws (\S8,
pp.~14--15).  A missing carrier or undefined observable pullback is a blocked
transfer, not a small numerical error.

\section*{Composition and persistence}

For compatible stochastic transfers, state kernels compose forward and
observable pullbacks compose in reverse.  Observation defects have a
contraction-weighted bound; directed deficiencies satisfy a triangle
inequality; deterministic equation residuals propagate only with a compatible
equation-space map (\S7.1, pp.~13--14; \S15, pp.~23--24).  Incompatible clocks,
interfaces, resources, or boundary conditions make the composite undefined.

Persistence is defined through induced path laws (\S9, pp.~15--17).
Endpoint return is insufficient.  Every required prefix must remain within
operational-identity, viability, residual, deficiency, and certificate
budgets.  ``A persistent object is a recursively maintained finite
boundary-state'' is therefore a target-relative organizing principle.  Its
promotion to a universal physical law remains conjectural, untested, and
unexecuted.

\section*{Eight fixtures}

The exact fixtures (\S16, pp.~24--27) are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item torus winding \((2,-3)\), with electric charge blocked without a physical
bridge;
\item distinct one-dimensional conductivities with one Dirichlet-to-Neumann
map;
\item binary experiments with directed deficiencies \(0\) and \(1/4\);
\item Koopman residual \(\sqrt{2/N}\) separated from polluted zero
eigenvalues;
\item a finite \(\mathbb Z_2\) frame transformation that does not descend
through the stated reduced quotient;
\newpage
\item locally nonempty parity constraints with no global section;
\item the off-shell residual \(m^2c\) of a massive-field shift; and
\item an exact receipt for \(x=-1\) refuting \(\sqrt{x^2}=x\).
\end{enumerate}
Fixtures 1--7 have mathematical derivations but no separate execution
receipts.  Fixture 8 has a deterministic CPython receipt.  The package is a
preprint with audit artifacts, not a complete reference implementation.

\section*{Physical promotion and the test}

Winding and physical charge are different typed objects (\S13, p.~21).  A
charge claim needs a derived bridge from a gauge representation, action and
current, Gauss law, anomaly condition, boundary condition, or calibrated
measurement.  Primordial-field toroidal models are therefore treated as
candidate topological and stabilization grammar, not an established derivation
of quark charge, QCD, or hadron dynamics (\S17, pp.~28--30).

Claims carry separate mathematical, empirical, computational, source, and
transfer statuses (\S2.2, p.~5).  Demotion propagates only along declared
dependency axes.  A theorem does not raise empirical status; a hash establishes
identity rather than truth; a proof DAG is not a proof; and an unexecuted
calculation remains unexecuted.

The appropriate test is adversarial and narrow: Does an equivalent
compositional record already exist?  Is a map ill-typed?  Does a composition
law fail?  Is there a counterexample to a fixture?  Does the record change no
scientific decision?  If existing formalisms already supply the same
obligations and demotion semantics, or if BSC never separates an invalid
transfer from a nearby valid one, the added structure is unnecessary.  If it
exposes that distinction and preserves it through composition, that---not a
claim about the universe---is the contribution requiring further work.
