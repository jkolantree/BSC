# Contributing

The preferred contribution is narrow, inspectable, and capable of changing a
status entry.

Use the repository issue forms for:

- prior-art equivalence;
- a type, proof, or hypothesis error;
- a fixture or reproduction failure; or
- a scope, source-status, or novelty correction.

Please identify the exact definition, theorem, fixture, page, equation, or
claim-ledger row. A useful report states the current claim, the objection, the
smallest supporting argument or source, and the status change that would follow
if the objection is correct.

Do not submit:

- general endorsement;
- claims that BSC proves an ontology or fundamental physical theory;
- new application claims without their typed bridge and evidence;
- generated prose presented as independent review; or
- numerical outputs without code, inputs, environment, tolerances, and a
  retained receipt.

Corrections are expected to be visible. A material repair should update the
manuscript or revision memorandum, the claim-status ledger, affected hashes,
and the changelog. A changed proposition receives a new claim identifier rather
than silently inheriting the old status.
